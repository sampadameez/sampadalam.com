The Story

A life shaped by
curiosity and courage.

I grew up in Calcutta, India — a city alive with energy, complexity, and ideas. That environment gave me something no classroom could: the ability to navigate uncertainty and find opportunity in chaos.

After completing my engineering degree, I spent five years honing my craft in India’s technology sector. Then at 27, I made the leap — packing up everything for Charlotte, North Carolina, in search of a bigger stage.

In 2022, the next chapter opened: I moved to Canada as a permanent resident, founded my own company, and began building products on my own terms — while continuing to consult for organizations navigating digital transformation.

Three countries. Countless lessons. One consistent north star: build things that work.

The Journey

A map drawn
one move at a time.

The Beginning
🇮🇳Calcutta, India
Raised in one of India’s most culturally rich cities, I grew up surrounded by a love of science, sport, and stories. Engineering at university opened the door to technology — and I never looked back.
First Career Chapter
🇮🇳India — Technology Sector
Five years of deep technical and delivery work across enterprise projects. I learned how large organizations move — and how to make them move faster. This is where the foundation was built.
The Big Move
🇺🇸Charlotte, North Carolina
Moving to the United States at 27 was equal parts terrifying and thrilling. Charlotte became my home base for years of growth — leading delivery teams, driving automation, and migrating legacy systems for enterprise clients.
2022 — Present
🇨🇦Canada — Founder & Builder
Canada brought a new identity: permanent resident, founder, and independent builder. Today I run my own company — creating products, consulting on transformation, and writing the most exciting chapter yet.
